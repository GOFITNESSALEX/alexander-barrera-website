Privacy Notice

Last updated: July 28, 2026.

Information collected

When you apply for coaching, we collect the details you provide, including your name, email address, phone number, goals, training preferences, and exercise-readiness answers.

How information is used

We use this information to respond to your application, assess whether coaching may be appropriate, communicate about services, schedule calls, and provide coaching if you become a client.

Service providers

Application data is submitted through Formspree. Scheduling is provided through Calendly. These providers process information according to their own privacy terms. Do not include information that you do not want to share through these services.

Health information

Exercise-readiness answers are requested only to help identify when medical guidance may be appropriate. They are not a diagnosis, and you should not submit detailed medical records through this website.

Sharing and retention

GO FITNESS ALEX does not sell personal information. Information may be shared only with service providers needed to operate coaching services, when required by law, or with your consent. Information is retained only as long as reasonably needed for these purposes.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your information, subject to legal and service requirements, by emailing ajbzfit@gmail.com. You may also opt out of non-essential marketing communications at any time.
